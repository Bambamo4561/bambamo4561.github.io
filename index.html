💡 What Is lidfx?

lidfx is a beautifully crafted extension for GNOME Shell (the interface on many Linux laptops) that gives you a visually stunning experience when you close your laptop's lid. As the lid lowers, your live desktop doesn't just turn off — it gracefully folds and blurs in a realistic, smooth animation. Think of it like a digital curtain falling away, powered by your computer's built-in motion sensor (accelerometer).

Instead of a harsh cutoff or a blank screen, you'll see your windows and wallpaper softly fold downward while a gentle blur effect takes over, until the screen finally rests. It's a small touch that makes closing your laptop feel modern, polished, and incredibly satisfying.

⚙️ Key Features

📐 Realistic Fold Effect

The desktop literally folds in half like paper, using GPU-accelerated GLSL shaders for a buttery-smooth visual illusion.

🌫️ Dynamic Blur

A high-quality blur effect kicks in as the lid closes, creating a soft, dreamy transition that looks natural and refined.

📡 Motion Sensing

Uses your laptop's HID accelerometer to detect the lid angle in real time, making the animation perfectly sync with your actual movement.

🔧 Seamless Integration

Sits right inside the GNOME Shell compositor (Mutter) for minimal performance impact and native-feeling behavior on Wayland.

🖥️ What You Need

Before downloading, make sure your setup matches these requirements:

  • ✅ A laptop running a modern Linux distribution (Ubuntu 22.04 or newer, Fedora 38+, or similar)
  • ✅ GNOME Shell version 42 or higher
  • ✅ Running on Wayland session (not X11)
  • ✅ A laptop with a built-in accelerometer (most modern ultrabooks and notebooks have one)
  • ✅ A GPU that supports OpenGL 3.3 or higher (almost all integrated graphics since 2015 work fine)

🛠️ Common Issues & Tips

  • 🔹 No effect happening? Make sure you're on a Wayland session. Log out, click the gear icon on the login screen, and select "Ubuntu on Wayland."
  • 🔹 Animation feels janky? Check that your graphics drivers are up to date. On Ubuntu, run sudo ubuntu-drivers install in a terminal.
  • 🔹 Accelerometer not detected? Some older laptops lack this sensor. lidfx gracefully falls back to a timed animation if no sensor is found, so you'll still get the effect.
